Commercial Projects

In short

Curtain walling, windows, doors and entrances for offices, industrial units, business parks, leisure buildings and self storage. Delivered as a package to the main contractor's programme, from the drawing office through our factory to site.

Commercial work is where the drawing office earns its keep. An office or industrial unit gets its aluminium package set out from the architect's elevations, fabricated and powder coated in our own works, then delivered in the sequence the main contractor needs so that the follow on trades are not waiting on us. It covers everything from a business park unit to a leisure centre.
