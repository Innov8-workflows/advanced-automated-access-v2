About us

Advanced Automated Access Ltd

An aluminium and automatic access contractor based on Canvey Island in Essex, working UK wide on commercial, retail, education and healthcare buildings. Curtain walling, windows, doors, shopfronts, automatic entrances and shutters are drawn, made and installed by the same business.

That matters most when something goes wrong. A frame made in house can be corrected in house, and a door commissioned by our own engineer is a door those engineers already know when it comes up for a service visit two years later.

We have been trading since 2008 and our team carries a combined knowledge of over a hundred years in the trade. The drawing office, the 10,000 sq ft factory and our own powder coating paintline are all under one roof, which is why we can respond when a specification changes and why we deliver faster to market than our competition.
