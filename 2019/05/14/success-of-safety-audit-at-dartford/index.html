Success of Safety Audit at Dartford!

14 May 2019

As part of our ongoing checks, we were very happy to recently receive a high standard of safety audit for our client Readie Construction Ltd at Capacity Dartford . With only one action point required highlighted by our external auditors HSE Advisor , we were able to feedback to the Main Contractor and our own resource that we were maintaining a safe environment and working practice. AAA pride ourselves on continuing to keep up to date with current H&S requirements and maintain our CHAS , Safecontractor and Constructionline accreditations .
