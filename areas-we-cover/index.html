Areas we cover, UK wide

In short

We work UK wide. Our factory, drawing office and powder coating paintline are on Canvey Island in Essex, and our engineers are based throughout the country rather than working out of a single depot. More than half the projects on this site are outside the South East.

Because the aluminium is fabricated and finished at our own works, distance changes the delivery, not the job. A curtain walled elevation in Wolverhampton is set out, made and coated in exactly the same way as one in Basildon. For servicing and breakdowns our engineers are spread across the country so a call out is answered from wherever is nearest, not from Essex.
