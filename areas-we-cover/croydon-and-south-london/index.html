Aluminium glazing and automatic doors in Croydon and South London

In short

We design, make and install aluminium curtain walling, shopfronts, windows, doors, louvres, steel doors and roller shutters across Croydon and South London, and we service and repair automatic doors there. Everything is fabricated and powder coated in our own factory on Canvey Island.

Our work in Croydon and South London

Croydon is one of the places our work is most visible. We completed the Vauxhall dealership on the Purley Way and the Boxpark scheme in the town centre, and we have worked at Safestore in Carshalton and Shurgard at Herne Hill. Croydon has more 1960s and 1970s office stock than anywhere else in south London, and a great deal of it is being refurbished or converted rather than demolished, which means replacement curtain walling and window packages into existing structural openings. The Purley Way retail and trade strip is a steady source of shopfront, shutter and automatic entrance work.

Frequently asked questions

How often does an automatic door need servicing?

Twice a year. Automatic doors fall under the machinery regulations, and to conform to EN 16005 they should be maintained twice annually. Our service team provides six monthly visits planned in advance so the building is not caught out.

Servicing and repair

What does a service contract include?

Our service contract guarantees prioritised call outs at fixed rates and discounted spare parts, on top of the planned six monthly visits. We cover automatic doors, manual doors and roller shutters.

What a contract covers

Can you service doors you did not install?

Yes. A large part of our service work is on third party equipment. The first visit establishes what is on site and what condition it is in, and the schedule is set from there.

Servicing and repair contracts

What happens if a door breaks down?

Log a call and we will get an engineer to you. Contract customers get prioritised call outs at fixed rates. Our engineers are based throughout the UK rather than working out of one depot.

Are you accredited?

We hold CHAS Elite and CHAS SSIP certification under membership CHAS-110047, we are a Constructionline Silver member, registration 136785, and we are a member of the Automatic Door Installation Association, membership 100104. Our teams hold SMSTS, SSSTS, CSCS and Asbestos Awareness.
