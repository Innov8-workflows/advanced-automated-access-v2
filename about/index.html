About us

In short

Advanced Automated Access Ltd designs, manufactures, installs and maintains aluminium curtain walling, windows, doors and shopfronts, and we are experts in door automation and roller shutters. We were incorporated in June 2008 and work from a 10,000 sq ft factory on Canvey Island with our own drawing office and powder coating paintline.

AAA values

Attentive

Our team work diligently to exceed our customers' expectations and to deliver a reliable, professional service. We carry a combined knowledge of over a hundred years of working in our trade, and our product knowledge lets us tailor a solution to the environmental, aesthetic and budget requirements of the job.

Adaptable

With engineers based throughout the UK, our own drawing office on hand and the ability to make aluminium and automatic entrances in our 10,000 sq ft factory, we can respond to changing specifications effectively and resolve issues as they arise during a project. We pride ourselves on delivering faster to market than our competition.

Assured

All our engineers are certificated to their relevant skills. Our teams hold SMSTS, SSSTS, CSCS, Asbestos Awareness and ADIA accreditation, so we operate to current health and safety requirements. We are an approved Comar and Senior Architectural Systems fabricator, and an approved Assa Abloy and record automatic door installer.

Making it ourselves

The thing that separates us from most of the firms we compete with is that we are a fabricator, not just an installer. The drawing office, the factory and the paintline are all under one roof, so a change to a specification part way through a job is a conversation rather than a new lead time. It is also why we can say we deliver faster to market than our competition.

Working UK wide

Our works is in Essex but our engineers are based throughout the country, and 33 of the 74 projects recorded on this site are outside the South East. They run from Wolverhampton and Stoke-on-Trent to Crewe, Trafford Park, Lincoln, Oswestry, Portsmouth, Swindon and Plymstock. Because the aluminium is made and coated at our own works, distance changes the delivery rather than the job, and a service call is answered by whichever engineer is nearest.
